Resources

Catalogs, reanalysis fields, and community sites for checking atmospheric-river frequency, integrated vapor transport (IVT), and landfall precipitation in EAMxx.

Analysis data

  • ARTMIP catalogues

    Binary atmospheric-river flags from many detection algorithms applied to the same MERRA-2, ERA5, JRA-55, and CMIP fields. Tier 1 MERRA-2 catalogues for January 1980–June 2017 are on the NSF NCAR Geoscience Data Exchange.

  • ERA5 hourly data on single levels

    ECMWF global reanalysis from 1940 to present on a 0.25° grid. The vertical integrals of eastward and northward water-vapour flux are the IVT components used by most global atmospheric-river detectors.

  • MERRA-2 vertically integrated diagnostics

    NASA hourly column water and energy fluxes (M2T1NXINT) from 1980 to present, near 0.5° × 0.625°. The vapor-flux terms are the MERRA-2 source for IVT and for the ARTMIP Tier 1 catalogues.

  • Global Atmospheric Rivers Database, version 4

    Guan and Waliser tARget version 4 applied to ERA5: object masks and life-cycle tracks for 1940–2023 at 6-hour intervals and 0.25°. The data paper documents the files in the UCLA Global Atmospheric Rivers Dataverse.

  • Atmospheric River Reconnaissance observations

    Dropsonde, radiosonde, and related profiles from targeted flights over the Northeast Pacific, collected to sample atmospheric rivers before U.S. West Coast landfall.

Websites

  • Center for Western Weather and Water Extremes

    Scripps center for atmospheric-river science, extreme precipitation, and western water. Forecast products, the AR scale, and field campaigns are posted here.

  • IWV and IVT forecasts

    Integrated vapor transport from the GFS, GEFS, and ECMWF, with a U.S. West Coast landfall tool. CW3E labels these graphics for research use.

  • Atmospheric River Scale

    Categories 1–5 from how long IVT stays above 250 kg m⁻¹ s⁻¹ and the maximum IVT during the event (Ralph et al., 2019). GEFS and ECMWF ensemble views cover the coming week.

  • Atmospheric River Reconnaissance

    Research-to-operations flights that release dropsondes ahead of landfalling atmospheric rivers to improve forecasts at lead times of 1–5 days.

  • International Atmospheric Rivers Conference 2026

    Sixth IARC, 19–23 October 2026 in Porto, Portugal, with virtual plenaries. Registration for in-person and virtual attendance is open through 10 October 2026.

  • TempestExtremes

    Open software for detecting and tracking atmospheric rivers and other extremes in gridded climate-model output.

News

  • 30 Sep 2026 Model climate as context for atmospheric-river landslides in Southeast Alaska

    Nash et al. rank forecast IVT, low-level wind, and precipitation against each variable’s model climate. In the CW3E summary, about 90% of landslide days reached the 95th percentile in all three, compared with about 30% of non-landslide days.

  • 9 Sep 2026 IARC2026 program set for Porto

    The conference program, drawn from nearly 200 abstracts, has 80 talks, 36 lightning talks, and 72 posters, plus a science–stakeholder forum.

  • 26 Aug 2026 California atmospheric-river activity reconstructed to 1500

    Annual landfall counts, integrated water vapor, and IVT for northern, central, and southern California, used to examine how atmospheric-river activity changes the ENSO–precipitation relationship.

  • 16 Jul 2026 Convection at atmospheric-river genesis and later precipitation

    Naud et al. combine 10 years of GPM precipitation type with an atmospheric-river database and composite storms on the point of maximum IVT.

  • 15 Dec 2025 NCEI historical atmospheric-river record

    Slinskey et al. describe a global ERA5 record from 1940 to present, 6-hourly at 0.25°, that pairs tARget version 3 detection with the AR scale and is updated monthly. The notice states that NCEI will host the files.
