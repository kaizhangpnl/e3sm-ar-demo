About

ARM For Modelers supports sustained dialogue between the observational and atmospheric-modeling communities and provides a lightweight home for figures, movies, and useful data links.

Focus

  • Applying ARM observations in model evaluation and development.
  • Communicating model successes, limitations, and priority knowledge gaps.
  • Sharing practical software, analysis frameworks, and diagnostic tools.
